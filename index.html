Understanding prana

Your energy has a weather of its own

Some days it is clear. Other days it is clouded, still, or running low.

Prana is the Sanskrit word for life force, the vital energy that keeps body and mind going. Around the physical body is an energy body, often called the aura, with centres of energy known as chakras.

Stress, illness, grief and long busy seasons can leave that energy congested in some places and depleted in others. People often describe this as feeling drained, heavy, restless or simply out of tune.

Pranic healing works directly with the energy body. Nothing is touched and nothing is taken. The healer works a few inches away from you, clearing what is stale and replenishing what is low, so your body has better conditions for its own natural recovery.

Questions

Things people often ask

Do I need to believe in energy healing for it to help?

No special belief is needed. Come with an open mind, relax, and notice how you feel afterwards.

Is it safe if I am on medication or under treatment?

Pranic healing is non-touch and uses no substances, so it can sit alongside your treatment. It does not replace it. Please keep following your doctor's advice and do not stop any medicine without speaking to them.

How does distance healing work?

You choose a time and a quiet place. We speak briefly by phone or video, the session follows the same steps as in person, and we check in again at the end.

How long is a session, and how many will I need?

Length and the number of sessions depend on what you are working with. Monika will suggest a plan after your first conversation, and you are always free to decide.

What should I do after a session?

Drink water, rest if you can, and go easy on heavy meals for the rest of the day. Monika may share a short breathing practice or meditation to support you.
